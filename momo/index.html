Momo Reels Bot

Momo Reels Bot is a personal tool of the owner of the Instagram account @momo.vkruge and the Facebook Page «Momo в круге». It prepares short entertainment videos and publishes them as Reels to the owner's own Instagram account through the Instagram Graph API.

The tool is used only by its owner. It is not offered to other people and has no other users.
