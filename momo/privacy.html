Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Momo Reels Bot is a personal tool that publishes videos to its owner's own Instagram professional account (@momo.vkruge) linked to the owner's Facebook Page.

What data the app uses

  • An access token of the owner's own Facebook Page, used to publish Reels to the owner's Instagram account and to read that account's basic information.
  • Public information of public Instagram business and creator accounts (public video posts and their public view counts), read through the Business Discovery API.

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of any other people. It has no users other than its owner and no sign-in for anyone else.

Where data is stored

The access token and the app's settings are kept only in the owner's private storage. They are sent only to Meta's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
