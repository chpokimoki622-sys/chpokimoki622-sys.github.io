Data Deletion Instructions

Momo Reels Bot stores no personal data of anyone other than its owner.

To remove the app's access to a Facebook account, open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), select «Momo Reels Bot» and click «Remove». The access token stops working immediately and the app keeps no other data about the account.

For any other request, contact the owner through github.com/chpokimoki622-sys.
