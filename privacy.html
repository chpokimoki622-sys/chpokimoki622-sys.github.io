Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Clips Uploader is a personal desktop tool that uploads videos to its owner's YouTube channels.

What data the app uses

When you sign in with Google, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos to your YouTube channel and read your channel name. It does not read your email, contacts or other Google data.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and settings are stored only on your own computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the token file from your computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/chpokimoki622-sys.
